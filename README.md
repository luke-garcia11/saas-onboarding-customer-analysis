# saas-onboarding-customer-analysis
Business analysis case study: investigating why customers onboard slowly at a fictional B2B SaaS company and how onboarding relates to churn. SQL, Power BI, process mapping, project planning. Synthetic, AI-assisted data.
Customer Onboarding & Churn Analysis: a Business Analysis Case Study
This project analyzes onboarding performance for a fictional B2B SaaS company (Vantora Software) to understand what drives slow onboarding and whether it is associated with first-year churn. It includes SQL analysis, a Power BI dashboard, current- and future-state process maps, requirements, and a project plan.
The company and dataset are entirely fictional. The data was synthetically generated with AI assistance, and my use of AI is documented in ai-log/.
